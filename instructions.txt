The artifacts app allows you to manage collections of artifacts such as computers, stamps, motorcycles.  These artficact collections are made up of artifacts.  Each artifact can have one or more photograph or video taken of the artifact.  The artifact can have a list of attributes such as date acquired, cost, current value, manufacturer, and notes.  Each image can also have notes.  The app should allow multiple collections.  The app should be written in swift and run on the iphone, ipad and macintosh computer.  The datasets should be persisted locally and also synchronized to iCloud using iCloudKit.  Datasets should be exportable, at least on the mac platform.  Ask any clarifying questions if this is not clear.
